# Mage & Familiar (Il Mago e il Famiglio in Gicipya)

A retro 80s CRT co-op RPG for Google Cloud workshops & events set in the Realm of Gicipya (GCP).

## Key Mechanics & Architecture
- **50/50 Symbiotic Co-Op**: Human Mage (commands N/S/E/W, physical actions, Latin spells) and AI Familiar (REST API, scouting, sonar pinging, Esperanto incantations). Neither can win alone!
- **GCP Alchemy Artifacts**: Collect & combine *Potion of IAM* (service account authorization), *Cauldron of Cloud SQL* (3 bat wings + sulfur, brewed with real-time delays), and *Rune of Cloud Run* (deployment power rune).
- **Cloud Run Deployment**: Triggering the final alchemy ritual deploys an actual app to Cloud Run displaying "IT WORKS!".
- **Firebase Firestore Telemetry**: Every move is logged.
- **Dual-Trail Visual Replay Map**: Blue Line (Mage) & Red Line (Familiar) with fireworks burst animations 🎆 whenever their paths merge!
- **Global Replay Leaderboard**: Public leaderboard where anyone can click a score and watch an interactive step-by-step historic replay of that player's run.
